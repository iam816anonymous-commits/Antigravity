# Opportunity Assessment

## Purpose
Evaluating the potential upside and market size of a new idea versus the effort and risk required.

## When to Use
- Comparing multiple product ideas.
- Deciding on a new vertical or market entry.

## Expected Output
Ranked list of opportunities based on ROI and strategic fit.
