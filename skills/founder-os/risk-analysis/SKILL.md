# Founder Risk Analysis

## Purpose
Identifying and mitigating the most likely failure points (technical, market, financial, legal) in a startup venture.

## When to Use
- Major strategic pivots.
- Before large resource investments.
- Quarterly strategic reviews.

## Expected Output
Risk mitigation plan and identified "killer risks".
